What Recruits Want to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the acceptance rate?

Typically 8–10%. Once admitted, Midshipmen graduate at a high rate — 98% retention through commissioning.

What is the "Two-Year Test Drive"?

The Academy offers Midshipmen the opportunity for two free years of education without committing to Navy or USMC service — a genuine trial period to make sure it's the right fit before the commitment kicks in.

How much liberty do Midshipmen get?

Liberty is based on year and academic performance, plus normal holiday breaks and government holidays.

What are the advantages of being on the team?
  • Brotherhood — camaraderie and guidance from upperclassmen
  • Athlete-focused scheduling and a healthier balance for athletic goals
What are the opportunities to compete?

Regardless of age or depth on the roster — 8 opens throughout the year.

What does "service" really mean here?

The Naval Academy offers the opportunity to be part of something bigger than oneself, and enables each Midshipman to serve in any capacity they so choose.
