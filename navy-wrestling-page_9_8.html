The Mission

We don't build wrestlers.
We build officers who happen to wrestle.

Led by Olympian Cary Kolat, Navy qualified 5 wrestlers to the 2023 NCAA Championships. The Midshipmen finished the season with a 3rd straight win over Army. A program with over 100 years of history and multiple NCAA Champions, the Naval Academy builds success.
